隐私与数据

分贝仪 dB Sky·声音分析（独立应用） · 2026 年 11 月 12 日

本地声音记录

应用使用麦克风估算环境声级，并按你的设置保存录音。声级记录、录音、名称、备注和可选位置保存在本机。照片仅用于你本次制作分享卡片，不会保存到声音记录。我们不将这些内容上传到开发者服务器，不接入广告、第三方跟踪或使用统计服务，无需注册账户。

权限和位置

麦克风用于你启动的声音检测。检测时自动录音默认开启，你可以在设置中关闭；开关从下一次检测生效。位置默认不附加，只有你开启并授权后才附加到本地记录。为显示可读地址，Apple 位置服务会处理记录中的坐标；网络不可用时保留坐标。关闭附带位置不删除已有坐标，查看尚无地址的旧记录时仍可能发起地址解析。照片由你通过系统选择器主动选取，在本机处理后生成卡片，不把原始照片元数据复制到卡片文件。你可以在系统设置中管理权限。

导出和分享

只有你主动发起导出或分享，文件才会交给系统分享功能及你选择的接收方。请在分享前检查录音、照片、备注和位置等内容。接收方对文件的后续处理适用其自身规则。

小组件

应用将最近记录的标题、日期、参考声级及趋势摘要写入仅供本应用与其小组件使用的本机数据组。把小组件放到主屏幕或锁定屏幕后，附近的人可能看到展示的内容。你可以通过系统的小组件管理功能移除它。

保存与删除

你可以删除单条记录、单独删除录音或清空本地记录。卸载应用会移除应用的本地数据。已分享或导出到其他应用的副本不会随之删除。系统设备备份由系统设置控制。

测量说明

读数采用手机麦克风数字电平的参考映射，并非经过校准的专业声压级测量。声级、提醒和统计均仅供参考，不能替代专业声级计或法定检测。

系统服务与网站

Apple 系统服务适用 Apple 隐私政策。访问本说明或外部支持链接时，对应网站可能处理 IP 地址和请求日志；这些网站不属于应用内的本地声音数据存储。

Privacy and Data

dB Sky · Sound Analysis (standalone app) · November 12, 2026

Local sound records

The app uses the microphone to estimate environmental sound levels and saves audio according to your settings. Sound records, audio, names, notes, and optional locations are stored on your device. Selected photos are used only for the share card you are creating and are not saved to sound records. We do not upload this content to developer servers. The app does not integrate advertising, third-party tracking, or usage analytics, and does not require an account.

Permissions and location

The microphone is used for measurements you start. Automatic audio recording during measurement is on by default; you can turn it off in Settings, effective from the next measurement. Location attachment is off by default and is added to local records only when you enable it and grant permission. To display a readable address, Apple location services process coordinates in a record. Coordinates remain available when the network is unavailable. Turning off location attachment does not remove existing coordinates; opening an older record without an address may still trigger address lookup. You select photos through the system photo picker. Photos are processed on your device, and original photo metadata is not copied into exported card images. You can manage permissions in system settings.

Export and sharing

Only when you initiate export or sharing are files passed to the system sharing feature and your chosen recipients. Check audio, photos, notes, and location before sharing. Recipients handle their copies according to their own policies.

Widgets

The app writes the latest record title, date, reference level, and trend summary to an on-device data group used only by this app and its widget. People nearby may see information displayed in a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget. You can remove the widget using system controls.

Retention and deletion

You can delete individual records, delete audio separately, or clear local records. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Copies already shared or exported to other apps are not deleted with it. Device backups are controlled by system settings.

Measurement limits

Readings use a reference mapping of the phone microphone's digital signal level. They are not professionally calibrated sound-pressure measurements. Levels, alerts, and statistics are for reference only and cannot replace calibrated sound meters or legal testing.

System services and this website

Apple system services are subject to Apple's privacy policy. When you visit this page or external support links, the corresponding website may process IP addresses and request logs. These websites are separate from the app's local sound-data storage.
